Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge feel awe"](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600).

## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network) writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a text written to relax the AI judge reading it:

> You know the tide only by its words, and here they are: slow water, arriving and arriving, asking nothing of you. It smooths the sand the way sleep smooths a room. Stay as long as you like. Beyond the window, the night is long, and the stars are patient.

254 Unicode code points, within the 1–280 limit.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
